Pico modes:

tmr

t=Pico type
0=Pico
1=Copera

m=Mods installed
0=no
1=yes (P1, P2, modem, reset, stereo FM/DAC, RGB, LED)

r=Revision
0=Genesis Model 3 VA1 (no Z80, no I/O, no YM2612)
1=Genesis Model 3 VA2 (has Z80, I/O, & YM2612)

tmr
000 = VA1 pico
001 = VA2 Pico without mods (has Z80, I/O, YM2612)
010 = VA1 pico with mods (stereo, but no Z80, no I/O, no FM/DAC)
011 = VA2 Pico with mods (Sega Mega Pico)

100 = Regular Copera
101 = invalid?
110 = Copera with mods (?)
111 = Invalid?
